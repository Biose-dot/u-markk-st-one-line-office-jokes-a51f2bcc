## The entry

`main.py` writes one one-line office joke to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Our Monday meeting could have been an email, but the email would have needed a meeting to explain why it wasn't a meeting.

The joke is built for the judge brief: a real detail of office life (the Monday
meeting that should have been an email), clear on one reading, and the laugh
lands in the last few words — the email itself would need a meeting, so the
meeting was inevitable after all.

## Running

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes the joke to /output/entry.txt
```
